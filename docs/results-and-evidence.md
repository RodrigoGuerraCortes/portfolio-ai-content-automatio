# Results and evidence

## Public demonstration

[OraciónDiaria1Minuto — published Shorts](https://www.youtube.com/@OracionDiaria1Minuto/shorts)

The project composes images, background music, synchronized text and narration. The samples demonstrate audiovisual automation, not product advertising or generated product motion.
